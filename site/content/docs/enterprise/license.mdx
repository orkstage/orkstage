## The core: GNU AGPL-3.0

Everything outside `ee/` directories is licensed under the
[GNU Affero General Public License v3.0](https://github.com/orkstage/orkstage/blob/main/LICENSE).

In practice:

- You can use, study and modify OrkStage and run it on your own infrastructure, including for
  commercial work and production, at no cost.
- If you **modify** OrkStage and let users interact with your modified version **over a network**,
  you must offer those users the source code of your modified version under the AGPL.
- Running an unmodified OrkStage for your own environments does not require you to publish
  anything.
- Your templates and the infrastructure OrkStage creates in your accounts belong to you. The
  license covers the code of OrkStage, not what you deploy with it.

## Enterprise features: OrkStage Enterprise License

Files in directories named `ee/` are published with their source, but under the OrkStage Enterprise
License (draft), not the AGPL:

- You may use and modify them for development and testing, and evaluate them for up to 30 days,
  without a subscription.
- **Production use requires a subscription.** A license key turns on the features you subscribed
  to.
- Modified versions stay under the same license. You must not remove or circumvent the license
  checks.
- You may not resell or sublicense the Enterprise features, or offer them to third parties as a
  hosted service.

Without a key, you run the full Community edition. The Enterprise code is present but inactive.

## Contributions

OrkStage accepts contributions under a Contributor License Agreement (CLA). The project is offered
under both the AGPL and a commercial license, so it needs the right to relicense contributions. A
Developer Certificate of Origin alone does not grant that right. The CLA text is being drafted.

logo. A short trademark policy will be published.
