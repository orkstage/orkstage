## The core: GNU AGPL-3.0

Everything outside `ee/` directories is licensed under the
[GNU Affero General Public License v3.0](https://github.com/orkstage/orkstage/blob/main/LICENSE).

In practice:

- You can use, study, modify and self-host OrkStage, including for commercial work and production,
  at no cost.
- If you **modify** OrkStage and let users interact with your modified version **over a network**,
  you must offer those users the source code of your modified version under the AGPL.
- Running an unmodified OrkStage for your own environments does not require you to publish anything.
- The infrastructure OrkStage creates in your accounts, and your templates, are yours; the license
  covers OrkStage's code, not what you deploy with it.

## Enterprise features: OrkStage Enterprise License

Files in directories named `ee/` are **source-available** under the OrkStage Enterprise License
(draft):

- you may use and modify them for development and testing, and evaluate them for up to 30 days,
  without a subscription;
- **production use requires a subscription**; a license key enables the features you subscribed to;
- modified versions stay under the same license, and license checks must not be removed or
  circumvented;
- you may not resell, sublicense or offer the Enterprise features as a hosted service to third
  parties.

Without a key you run the full Community edition; Enterprise code is present but inactive.

## Contributions

Contributions are accepted under a Contributor License Agreement (CLA). Because the project is
offered under both the AGPL and a commercial license, it needs the right to relicense contributions;
a Developer Certificate of Origin alone does not grant that. The CLA text is being drafted.

## Trademarks

logo. A short trademark policy will be published.
