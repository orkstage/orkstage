---
title: Community vs Enterprise
description: Compare the OrkStage Community edition (AGPL-3.0) with the commercial Enterprise edition (ee/) area by area, with the status of each Enterprise item.
---

**Community** is complete. It includes templates, multicloud placement, plans, policies and cost,
deployments, health, incidents, status pages, notifications, the audit log, API keys, webhooks, CI
intake, IaC import and the CLI. **Enterprise** adds features for organizational scale and
compliance.

Enterprise items marked <Badge tone="planned">planned</Badge> are on the roadmap but not built yet.

| Area | Community (AGPL-3.0) | Enterprise (`ee/`) |
|---|---|---|
| Provisioning | Templates and the visual builder; AWS through OpenTofu; simulated Azure and OCI; DAG with retries; provider plans before apply; drift scans | Policy packs (rule sets in the style of CIS and SOC 2) <Badge tone="planned">planned</Badge>; custom policy authoring <Badge tone="planned">planned</Badge>; cost budgets per team <Badge tone="planned">planned</Badge> |
| Multicloud | Placement per resource; connectivity strategy for links between clouds | Provisioning of private connectivity (VPN, interconnect) <Badge tone="planned">planned</Badge> |
| Governance | 7 predefined roles; approvals for high risk production changes; audit log that only accepts inserts | **Audit log export** (NDJSON, CSV); custom roles and detailed permissions <Badge tone="planned">planned</Badge>; approvals with several steps and change windows <Badge tone="planned">planned</Badge>; audit retention policies and SIEM streaming <Badge tone="planned">planned</Badge> |
| Identity | Development login (local); OIDC with Microsoft Entra ID, Google and GitLab through configuration | SAML 2.0 SSO <Badge tone="planned">planned</Badge>; SCIM provisioning <Badge tone="planned">planned</Badge>; enforced SSO and MFA, session policies <Badge tone="planned">planned</Badge> |
| Organizations | One organization, many tenants | Multiple organizations, delegated administration <Badge tone="planned">planned</Badge>; status pages with your own branding and custom status domains <Badge tone="planned">planned</Badge> |
| Integrations | API keys, webhooks, CI intake, IaC import, `ork` CLI (Preview) | Change management with ServiceNow or Jira <Badge tone="planned">planned</Badge>; PagerDuty and Opsgenie <Badge tone="planned">planned</Badge> |
| Observability | Health, incidents, private status pages, logs and metrics through adapters | Metrics retention over long periods, SLO management, incident analytics <Badge tone="planned">planned</Badge> |
| Support | Community (GitHub Discussions) | Support backed by an SLA, security advisories before public disclosure <Badge tone="planned">planned</Badge> |

One question is still open: whether future Azure, OCI and GCP adapters belong to Community or
Enterprise. The current recommendation is Community, so that multicloud stays in the open core.

## Editions

| Edition | For | Model |
|---|---|---|
| Community | Any size, on your own infrastructure | Free, AGPL-3.0 |
| Enterprise (on your own infrastructure) | Regulated or larger organizations | Annual subscription, support included |
