## Channels

| Channel | Status |
|---|---|
| In-app (the bell in the console header) | Available |
| Email (SMTP, through an outbox) | Available |
| [Webhooks](/docs/developer/webhooks/) (HMAC-signed HTTP POST) | Available |
| Slack, Microsoft Teams | Planned |

## Who receives what

Deployment and incident notifications go to the tenant's **owners and admins** and to **platform
admins**. Examples:

- a deployment succeeded or failed;
- a production deployment is waiting for approval;
- an incident opened, was updated or resolved.

Each in-app notification links to the deployment or incident. Mark it read from the bell; the API is
`GET /notifications` and `POST /notifications/:id/read`.

## Email

Emails are queued in an outbox and sent by the worker through SMTP (`SMTP_HOST`, `SMTP_PORT`,
`SMTP_SECURE`, `EMAIL_FROM`). In the local stack they land in Mailpit at http://localhost:8025.

## Not yet

- Per-user notification preferences.
- Slack and Teams channels (use a webhook receiver in the meantime).
