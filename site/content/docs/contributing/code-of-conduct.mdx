OrkStage adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)
as its code of conduct, in every project space: repositories, issues, pull requests, discussions and
events.

In short:

- Be respectful and constructive. Critique code and ideas, not people.
- Assume good intent, and ask before you assume.
- Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks and publishing others' private information are not
  tolerated.
- Maintainers may remove, edit or reject contributions that do not follow the code, and may ban
  participants who repeatedly or seriously violate it.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to [hello@orkstage.io](mailto:hello@orkstage.io). Reports are
handled confidentially. Security vulnerabilities go to
[security@orkstage.io](mailto:security@orkstage.io) instead.

## Contributions and the CLA
