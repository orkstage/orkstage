OrkStage adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)
as its code of conduct. It applies in every project space: repositories, issues, pull requests,
discussions and events.

In short:

- Be respectful and constructive. Critique code and ideas, not people.
- Assume good intent. Ask before you assume.
- We do not tolerate harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, or publishing someone else's
  private information.
- Maintainers may remove, edit or reject contributions that break the code. They may ban
  participants who violate it repeatedly or seriously.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to [conduct@orkstage.io](mailto:conduct@orkstage.io).
Maintainers handle every report in confidence.

Send security vulnerabilities to [security@orkstage.io](mailto:security@orkstage.io) instead.

## Contributions and the CLA

OrkStage is offered under both the AGPL-3.0 and a commercial license. For that reason, every
contribution is accepted under a Contributor License Agreement (CLA). See
[License](/docs/enterprise/license/#contributions).
